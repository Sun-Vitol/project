Our Story

ABOUT BLUSH CRUMB BAKERY

Founded with a deep passion for traditional baking and artisanal craftsmanship, Blush Crumb Bakery uses only the finest French butter, locally sourced organic flour, and timeless fermentation techniques. Every loaf of bread and delicate pastry is crafted daily from scratch to bring warmth, comfort, and unforgettable flavor to your table.

🌾 100% Organic Flour
🧈 Pure French Butter
⏰ 48-Hour Fermentation
❤️ Baked with Passion
